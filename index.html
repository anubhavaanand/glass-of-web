How it works

The effect rests on a single SVG filter primitive, feDisplacementMap. It reads a generated normal map to decide which way to push pixels of live rendered content.

Stage 1

Source Graphic

The unfiltered, live DOM element containing text, buttons, and graphics.

Stage 2

SDF Normals

Pre-calculated 8-bit displacement map encoding horizontal (R) and vertical (G) vectors.

Stage 3

Chromatic Split

3× feDisplacementMap passes with scaled factors (1.08, 1.04, 1.0) creating prism dispersion.

Stage 4

Hole Punch & Specular

Lens mask cuts the lens region over the background and overlays luminous physical highlights.

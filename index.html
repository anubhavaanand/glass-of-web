How it works: Each component uses backdrop-filter: blur(12px) with rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2) background on an absolutely-positioned glass layer. The content sits above it at z-index: 1. This is the exact technique used on Aave.com for their glass cards — no SVG filters needed.

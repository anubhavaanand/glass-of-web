How it works: The filter applies feDisplacementMap with three separate passes for R, G, B channels at different scales (1.2×, 1.0×, 0.8×) — creating chromatic aberration. A feFlood(128) + feImage + feComposite(over) builds the displacement map. backdrop-filter: blur() on a sibling frost layer adds the glassy blur. A CSS radial gradient creates the specular highlight.
